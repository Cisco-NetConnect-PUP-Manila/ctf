# Packet Capture Website

This context defines the product language for the Packet Capture website, a narrative landing experience for a networking and security CTF competition.

## Language

**Beneath the Network investigation**:
The website's canonical content direction: participants become a Cyber Incident Response Team investigating a hidden reality beneath ordinary network traffic.
_Avoid_: Generic cyber arena, abstract creative studio, generic tech conference, portfolio showcase

**Cyber Incident Response Team**:
The participant role in the story: investigators activated to uncover the source of anomalies, preserve recovered evidence, and connect fragments across the operation.
_Avoid_: Players, hackers, visitors

**Network core**:
The persistent 3D centerpiece that evolves as the user scrolls through the website, tying each section into one continuous investigation experience.
_Avoid_: Unrelated section decorations, static background object, generic 3D ornament

**Packet-capture network map**:
The visual form of the network core: glowing nodes, packet trails, rings, and glitch or scan pulses that transform from entry portal to protocol map, attack vectors, and flag capture flow.
_Avoid_: Space scene, abstract ribbon, decorative particles

**Act**:
A narrative phase of the competition. The four acts are The Signal, The Breach, The Echo, and Beneath the Network.
_Avoid_: Generic track, category, level

**Four Acts**:
The public section model for presenting the main challenge structure. It replaces separate Tracks and Challenges sections with one narrative sequence of acts plus a spoiler-safe Final Investigation teaser.
_Avoid_: Separate tracks and challenges sections, disconnected category grid

**Rules of Engagement**:
The concise in-universe practical guidance for participants. It explains sequence, unlocks, artifact preservation, scope boundaries, and fair play without turning the website into a dry rulebook.
_Avoid_: Long policy page, generic FAQ dump, out-of-world rule wall

**Final phrase**:
The hidden phrase assembled from preserved key fragments across the investigation. Public website content may tease its existence but must not reveal the exact phrase.
_Avoid_: Bonus answer, secret code, extra flag

**Final Transmission**:
The registration and closing CTA framing for the public website. It teases the post-Act IV investigation layer without exposing the final phrase or solution fragments.
_Avoid_: Generic register footer, solution reveal, ending explanation

**Spoiler-safe challenge metadata**:
Public challenge content may include act names, challenge names, point totals, categories, unlock requirements, and redacted fragment hints. Act point totals, challenge names, and unlock thresholds are public, but real flag strings and the ordered key fragments that solve the final phrase are not.
_Avoid_: Public flags, complete solution fragments, answer keys

**Brutal cyber editorial**:
The visual system that merges the existing cyber arena direction with a brutal/futuristic editorial feel: large confident sections, disciplined motion, readable typography, and sharp structural edges.
_Avoid_: Full game HUD everywhere, cramped decorative panels, random animation pileup
